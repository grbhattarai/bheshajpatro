Bheshaj Spiritual Center

Bheshaj Spiritual Center is a small personal effort rooted in Sanatana Dharma and Vedic traditions.

It is not an institution or a public teaching platform. It is a quiet space from which a few things are built and shared carefully with the community.

The name reflects the idea of something that restores: slowly, through right understanding and right practice.

What is offered here reflects long-term study and lived practice, not instruction or performance.

Bheshaj Panchanga is the Center’s primary public offering. Everything else remains private.

The intention is to grow slowly, stay grounded, and remain genuinely useful to those who find their way here.

Bheshaj Panchanga

Bheshaj Panchanga is a traditional luni-solar calendar built to support everyday awareness of tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise, sunset, eclipses, and related observances.

It is freely available as a public reference — no registration, no payment. The aim is to make traditional calendar knowledge accessible in a form that is careful, usable, and quietly maintained over time.

The Panchanga is a practical reference, not a media platform or teaching program.

Its ongoing development and maintenance is supported in part through Bheshaj Astrology, a separate Vedic Jyotish consulting practice. That work helps sustain the time and effort required to keep this public resource available and well maintained.

Founder

Bheshaj Spiritual Center was founded and is maintained by Dr. Gandhi R. Bhattarai — an economist by profession and a student of Vedic Jyotish and Sanatana Dharma for over fifteen years.

His work reflects a long engagement with both analytical thought and traditional study, approached with patience, discipline, and care.

As a cancer survivor, his experience brought unusual clarity to what truly matters. Bheshaj reflects a personal commitment to practice and community, along with a quiet intention to dedicate more time, learning, and resources to this path in the years ahead.

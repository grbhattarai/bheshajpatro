What is Bheshaj

Bheshaj is a small personal effort rooted in Sanatana Dharma and Vedic traditions. It is not an institution or a public platform. It is a quiet space from which a few things are built and shared carefully, with Bheshaj Panchanga as the main public resource. The name reflects the idea of something that restores — slowly, through right understanding and right practice — and what is shared here reflects long-term study and lived practice, not instruction or performance.

Bheshaj Panchanga

Bheshaj Panchanga is a traditional luni-solar calendar created to support everyday awareness of tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise, sunset, eclipses, and related observances. It is freely available as a public reference, without registration or payment. The Panchanga is intended as a practical reference, not as a media platform or teaching program. The aim is to make traditional calendar knowledge accessible in a form that is careful, usable, and quietly maintained over time.

Ongoing development and maintenance of Bheshaj Panchanga is supported through Bheshaj Astrology, a related area of practice offering traditional Vedic Jyotish consultations. That work helps sustain the time and effort required to keep public resources available and well maintained. Bheshaj is meant to grow slowly and remain grounded. The intention is not to build a large platform, but to continue offering work that is useful, thoughtful, and aligned with long-term service to community.

About the Developer

Bheshaj was built and is maintained by Dr. Gandhi R. Bhattarai — an economist by profession and a student of Vedic Jyotish and Sanatana Dharma for over fifteen years. His work reflects a long engagement with both analytical thought and traditional study, approached with patience, discipline, and care.

As a cancer survivor, his experience brought unusual clarity to what truly matters. Bheshaj reflects a personal commitment to practice and community, along with a quiet intention to dedicate more time, learning, and resources to this path as the years ahead allow.

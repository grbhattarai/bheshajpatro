Bheshaj Panchanga

Bheshaj Panchanga is a traditional luni-solar calendar for everyday reference, including tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise, sunset, eclipses, and related observances.

It is freely available, with no registration or payment required.

The Panchanga was developed by Gandhi R. Bhattarai, PhD. Its development and maintenance are supported in part by Bheshaj Astrology.

Bheshaj Spiritual Center

Bheshaj Spiritual Center is a personal space for spiritual practice, study, and reflection, rooted in Sanatana Dharma and Vedic traditions. It shares writing and reference resources; the Panchanga is one of its public resources.
